Choices the specification leaves open, logged as required by `DMI_BRD_for_Claude_Code.md` section 0, rule 3. Newest milestone last.

Building a standalone app on the reference stack (BRD 4.2), mounted at `APP_BASE_PATH` and ready to swap its auth for BEAM's later. | Step 1 of the detection procedure found no BEAM application. |

## M1 Foundations

| Date | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-09-30 | **Versions added:** PostgreSQL 16.15 (Homebrew locally, `postgres:16` in CI, `postgres:16-alpine` in compose), Drizzle ORM 0.45.3 with drizzle-kit 0.31.11, node-postgres 8.23.0, Auth.js (`next-auth`) 5.0.0-beta.32, Zod 4.6.5, tsx 4.23.15. Auth.js v5 is still published under the beta tag; it is the version that supports the App Router and Next.js 16. | BRD 4.2 asks for exact versions. |
| 2026-09-30 | **Three database roles** (`db/bootstrap.sql`): `dmi` owns the schema and runs migrations; `dmi_app` is the application login and is always subject to row-level security; `dmi_system` has `BYPASSRLS` and no login. `dmi_app` may `SET LOCAL ROLE dmi_system` inside one transaction. Adds `DATABASE_MIGRATE_URL` (owner connection) to the environment variables in BRD 20.1; it falls back to `DATABASE_URL`. | Row-level security must apply to the application, but sign-in, the scheduler and seeding have to look across workspaces before a workspace is chosen. A separate role keeps that path explicit and auditable. |
| 2026-09-30 | **Query layer** (`src/db/client.ts`) exports only `withWorkspace(id, fn)` and `withSystem(reason, fn)`. The pool and Drizzle instance are private, so an unscoped query cannot be written. `withWorkspace` refuses anything that is not a UUID. `withSystem` reasons are limited to `identity`, `scheduler`, `seed`, `platform_admin` and `test`. | BRD 5.1: "a query layer that refuses any query without a workspace scope". |
| 2026-09-30 | **Row-level security** is one policy per table, `workspace_id = nullif(current_setting('app.workspace_id', true), '')::uuid`, enabled and forced, written in the hand-authored migration `0001_rls_and_grants.sql`. `workspaces` filters on `id`; `users` shows only members of the current workspace. An integration test fails if any table in schema `dmi` lacks the policy. | BRD 5.1. The test guards against a future migration forgetting the block. |
| 2026-09-30 | **Enums are PostgreSQL enums** throughout, in schema `dmi`. | BRD 6: "choose one approach and use it everywhere". |
| 2026-09-30 | **`relationship_scores` key:** a unique constraint on (`workspace_id`, `person_id`, `user_id`) with `NULLS NOT DISTINCT`, plus a surrogate `id`, instead of a primary key. The same `NULLS NOT DISTINCT` is used for the `roles` and `connections` unique keys, whose columns can be null. | A primary key cannot contain the null `user_id` that BRD 6.4 uses for firm-level scores. |
| 2026-09-30 | **Schema additions beyond BRD 6:** `workspaces.allowed_email_domains` (BRD 5.2 allow-list); `users.is_platform_admin` and `users.last_amr` (BRD 5.2 `amr` claim); `person_interests` (BRD 14 and 8.6 interests with sources); `capture_exclusions` (BRD 11.3 per-user exclusion list); `signals.confirmed_at` (the goal metric in BRD 1 reads it); `actions.company_id`, `actions.headline` and `actions.blocked_reason`, with `actions.person_id` nullable (BRD 12.1 "Find the right contact at <company>" and BRD 12.2 blocked notes); `content_items.description` (BRD 17.3 draft input); `queue_items.status` values `pending` and `resolved`; `audit_log.workspace_id` nullable for platform-level entries. | Each is required by a later section of the BRD but has no column in section 6. |
| 2026-09-30 | **`updated_at`** is maintained by the ORM (`$onUpdate`), not a database trigger. | BRD 19: no business logic in the database beyond constraints and row-level security. |
| 2026-09-30 | **Domain allow-list sign-in** creates an `account_lead` membership (read-only) for someone whose email domain is allowed but who has no invitation. Invited members keep their invited role; the invitation becomes active on first sign-in. | BRD 5.2 does not say which role a domain sign-in gets; `account_lead` is the least privileged. |
| 2026-09-30 | **Sessions** are Auth.js JWTs re-issued at most every 15 minutes and expiring after 12 hours without a re-issue, which gives the 12-hour inactivity timeout. Every auth cookie and the workspace cookie are scoped to `APP_BASE_PATH` and named with a `dmi.` prefix. | BRD 5.2 and 3.2. |
| 2026-09-30 | **Mock sign-in bypass** is a credentials provider registered only when `DATA_MODE=mock`, listing active members of any workspace. It takes no password. | BRD 21: "sign-in bypass available only in mock mode". |
| 2026-09-30 | **Seed data** is generated deterministically (fixed seeds, dates relative to now) in `src/db/seed/generate.ts`. Company names are coined words; every email and domain is under `example.com`, `example.org` or `example.net`; LinkedIn URLs use an `example-` slug prefix. A second, smaller workspace, "Harbor Search Group" (5 companies, 12 people), exists for tenancy tests and the workspace switcher; the validator, Sam Okafor, belongs to both. | BRD 21. The second workspace is needed to prove isolation (acceptance scenario 23). |
| 2026-09-30 | **Seed shape:** 150 people = 123 executives + 18 board-only directors + 9 sponsor partners; 220 roles = 164 current + 56 ended. The 6 people without LinkedIn URLs are single-seat board directors. Interactions follow five profiles (strong, warm, cooling, going quiet, cold); a check against the BRD 11.4 formula gives every band, including none. One recent placement drives the function-level off-limits rule; one person in Germany has unknown email consent. | Supplies every case the later acceptance scenarios need. |
| 2026-09-30 | **API routes** live under `/api/v1` and are wrapped by `apiRoute(capability, handler)`, which requires a session, checks the BRD 5.4 capability, and hands the handler the workspace-scoped context. The generated tenancy test discovers every `route.ts` under `/api/v1` and calls each handler as a user of the other workspace; a route with a new dynamic segment fails the test until it is mapped. | BRD 22.1 asks for a generated test over every route. |
| 2026-09-30 | **Tests:** Vitest runs two projects: `unit` (jsdom) and `integration` (node, against `dmi_test`, rebuilt and seeded once per run). Playwright uses its own database, `dmi_e2e`, and signs in once per role in global setup. `/api/health` is a database-free liveness route used for readiness checks. | Keeps the three suites from resetting each other's data. |
| 2026-09-30 | **`docker compose up`** runs a one-shot `setup` service (migrations, then the seed if the database is empty in mock mode) before `web` starts. `db/bootstrap.sql` is mounted as the Postgres init script. Still not run locally, because Docker is not installed on this laptop. | Acceptance scenario 24. |
